## ✒️ About TINTA

**TINTA** is a creative lettering and calligraphy studio focused on handcrafted typography, artistic composition, and expressive visual communication.

The studio brings together traditional calligraphy techniques and contemporary lettering to create distinctive pieces for brands, personal projects, stationery, events, packaging, editorial design, and creative collections.

TINTA is built around the idea that lettering is more than writing.

It is **form, rhythm, movement, personality, and visual expression.**

---

## 🎨 Our Philosophy

At TINTA, every letter has character.

Our approach combines:

- ✒️ Traditional calligraphy

Visual presentation is an important part of the TINTA experience.

- Clean
- High quality
- Properly framed
- Centered when appropriate
- Fully visible
- Consistent in composition
- Free of unnecessary interface elements
- Free of embedded prices
- Free of buttons
- Free of website screenshots
- Visually coherent with the TINTA identity

Photography and artwork should communicate the tactile feeling of paper, ink, brushes, pens, and handcrafted typography.
